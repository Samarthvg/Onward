    One-click autofill on application forms

    This works as a special bookmark. Instead of opening a page, clicking it fills in the form you're looking at with the details above. It never submits anything.

    1. Show your bookmarks toolbar: press Ctrl+Shift+B (Firefox, Chrome and Edge).
    2. Drag this green button onto the toolbar:
      Fill application
      Dragging doesn't work? Click Copy bookmark, right-click the bookmarks toolbar, choose Add Bookmark…, name it "Fill application", paste into the URL field, and save.
    3. On a job's application form (the Apply ↗ button opens one), click the bookmark. Then attach your resume, answer the remaining questions, review, and submit yourself.

    Works on Greenhouse and Ashby forms and on Workday. Workday asks you to sign in first, and each company's Workday site needs its own account; make it yourself, then click the bookmark on each step (My Information, My Experience). It never clicks Next or answers sponsorship, work authorization, or demographic questions.

    Try it here first

    Changed your details? Make the bookmark again; it carries the details from when it was made.
