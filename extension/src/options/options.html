Work authorization and standard answers

Sponsorship and work authorization questions are only answered when you set an answer here. Leave one on "Ask me each time" and the form will leave it for you.

Voluntary self-identification

Demographic questions (gender, race, veteran, disability). "Decline to answer" picks the form's decline option; if a form has none, the question is left for you.

Drafting answers

For questions like "Why do you want to work here?", the panel can draft an answer from your master resume and the job posting, using the Onward helper on this computer. Your ChatGPT or Claude login costs nothing extra; an OpenAI or Anthropic API key you added in the Onward settings is billed per draft and stops at its monthly budget. Drafts go into the field for you to edit; nothing is sent to the employer.

    What autofill will never do

    • Press Submit, Next, Apply or Save and Continue, or submit a form in any way.
    • Create an account or sign in.
    • Answer sponsorship, work authorization or demographic questions without an answer saved here.
    • Answer salary, consent, signature, background-check or legal questions.
    • Fill a field whose label it can't tie to that one field.
    • Overwrite something already typed in a field.
